Overview

A screen worth spending the day with.

The Zenbook 14 OLED is for people who spend the day in front of a screen. The 3K OLED panel is sharp, colourful and genuinely enjoyable for work and video, while the 1.2 kg body is easy to carry between home, office and campus.

Intel's Core Ultra 7, 16 GB of memory and a 1 TB SSD handle heavy browsing, Office and photo editing comfortably. The glossy screen can show reflections in bright rooms and ports are limited, so a small USB-C hub is a sensible extra. For heavy 3D work or gaming, choose a performance laptop instead.

Good for

  • Professionals who work on the move
  • Writing, design and photo editing
  • Long days away from a charger
  • Anyone who values a great screen
