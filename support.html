03 / Questions

Before you call.

A few practical answers. If yours is not here, call or send a message — we will tell you what we need to know.

Ask a question
What should I bring when I drop off a device?

Bring the device, its charger and any accessories needed to reproduce the problem. If you can, back up important files first, and tell us what happened and when it started.

Do you repair devices you did not sell?

Yes — support is not limited to equipment supplied by BTS. We assess the device and explain whether a repair makes sense before any work begins.

Do you visit homes and offices?

Yes. We are based in Mahendranagar, Kanchanpur. Call first so we can understand the work, confirm the visit and agree a time.

How long will a repair take?

It depends on the fault and parts availability. We confirm the likely timeline after diagnosis and tell you if anything changes.

What does an AMC cover?

Annual maintenance is a planned support arrangement for an organisation’s systems. The covered equipment, visit frequency and terms are agreed in writing after we review the setup.

What about warranty on supplied products?

Manufacturer warranty terms apply. We explain them at handover and help with the practical steps if a claim is needed.

04 / Before you callUseful details

A little detail saves time.

You do not need to diagnose the problem. These details help us do it faster — whether you call, message or bring the device in.

  • 01
    Device or system

    Make, model and operating system if you know them.

  • 02
    What happens

    The error message, sound, restart or failure — and when it started.

  • 03
    What changed

    New software, a move, a power cut or a new device on the network.

  • 04
    Backups

    Whether important files are backed up. If not, tell us before any work begins.

  • 05
    Access

    For on-site work, who can let us in and what equipment or rooms we need to see.

Something not working?

Send a message with the device or system and what it is doing. We will tell you the next step.
