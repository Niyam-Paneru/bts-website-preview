Overview

When the work needs real power.

The TUF Gaming F15 is built for performance first. An Intel H-series Core i7 and NVIDIA RTX graphics give it the headroom for modern games, video editing, 3D work and heavy multitasking, while the 144 Hz screen keeps movement smooth.

That power comes with honest trade-offs: it is heavier than an everyday laptop, battery life is shorter while gaming, and the fans are audible under load. If you want one machine for work and play and do not mind carrying it, it is the strongest performer of the three.

Good for

  • Modern games at high settings
  • Video editing and 3D work
  • Engineering and design software
  • A laptop that can replace a desktop
