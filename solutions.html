Scope first. Then supply.

No two sites are the same. The sequence stays simple, and nothing starts until the work and cost are clear.

01

Talk it through

What works, what fails and what has to keep running. A short conversation is enough to start.

02

Look at the site

Where a visit is needed, we look at the space, the existing equipment and the practical constraints.

03

Equipment, work and cost are confirmed before anything is ordered or installed.
