01Who we are
BTS / Mahendranagar

Built around a simple idea.

At BTS — Best Tech Solutions, we believe technology should do more than work — it should work reliably, efficiently, and securely. Based in Mahendranagar, Kanchanpur, Nepal, we provide practical technology solutions designed to meet the needs of individuals, businesses, and institutions.

From computer hardware repair and networking infrastructure to CCTV security systems and IT maintenance, we bring technical expertise and a detail-oriented approach to every project. Our focus is on understanding each customer’s needs, identifying the right solution, and delivering dependable service with professionalism and care.

We are not a distant vendor or a call centre. We are your neighbours — close enough to visit, accountable enough to call back, and experienced across the systems your organisation depends on.

“The relationship continues after the installation or repair.
That is the job.”
